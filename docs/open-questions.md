| Q-I19 | What does Vector Search do? | Parsed documents are split into chunks along their sections, each with its document id and character offsets, and embedded into an IRIS SQL `VECTOR` column next to `case_id`. Case-scoped queries are an **exact scan** filtered by `case_id` (tens of chunks per case; an approximate HNSW index combined with a filter can miss rows); HNSW is at most for the MKN-10 table. Used for: (1) a quote not found verbatim is flagged and shown with the nearest chunk, never accepted (D-2 unchanged); (2) **"ask the case"**: retrieve the top 8 chunks, each **widened to its whole document** while the excerpts stay under 12 000 characters (a report too long to fit keeps only its best chunks; changed 17. 10.: a broad question like "Jaký byl nález na CT?" matches a report's header best while the answer sits in a later section, measured offline on the reference case in `packages/ai/tests/test_ask_eval.py`), the model answers only with quotes, every quote is checked verbatim like extraction, and the answer is "not found" when no quote passes (no similarity cut-off, scores aren't calibrated across models); (3) MKN-10 code candidates (Q-C11). Duplicate uploads are **not** a vector-search job (Q-I22) | data-model.md › Non-FHIR storage, slices.md S8 |
| Q-I20 | What FHIR exchange is in the MVP? | **Out:** the MDT conclusion as a FHIR **document `Bundle`** per the FHIR R4 rules: `Composition` first, then only the resources it references (`Patient`, `Practitioner`s, `Condition`, results, …); a unique `Bundle.identifier` and a timestamp; never changed once assembled, so a corrected conclusion is a new Bundle with a new identifier. A separate **`DocumentReference`** indexes it, with two attachments: the Bundle and the PDF. Tasks, including request tasks, projected as FHIR `Task` (requester = KOC, owner = whoever holds the report; the pattern of the Da Vinci CDex guide). **In:** a referral accepted as a FHIR `Bundle` next to file upload (open detail: Q-I21). Exchange writes go through the outbox (D-14). HL7v2 is out (Q-I18) | data-model.md, slices.md S9 |
| Q-I22 | How are duplicate uploads detected? | **Not by embeddings**: similar reports from one department share a template and would look like duplicates. A **SHA-256 hash** of the bytes catches exact copies; **word-shingle overlap** (Jaccard) of the parsed text catches near copies, e.g. the same report sent by the GP and by the facility. A match is a warning, never a blocked upload | slices.md S2 |
